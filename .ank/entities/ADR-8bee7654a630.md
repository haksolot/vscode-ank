Everything reaching the user from ank is English: entity titles as their
authors wrote them, `check` findings, and refusals in the form
`error[4]: TASK-6da1 held by tool/1.0`. Those are data and pass through
untranslated.

A French shell around English data is a seam in the middle of every view — a
label in one language sitting above the sentence it introduces in another. One
language throughout is the smaller cost, and English is the one the data has
already chosen.
